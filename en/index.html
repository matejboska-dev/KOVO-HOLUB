About Us

A family engineering workshop established in 1927, now led by the third generation of the Holub family.

We manufacture heavy-duty skip & roll-off containers, steel and aluminium commercial truck bodies, and bespoke steel structures. In-house fabrication in Nepomuk (Czech Republic) with no intermediaries — serving clients in the Netherlands, Germany, France, Austria and the Czech Republic.
