100 Years Story · 1927–2027

Three generations, one family workshop, 1,400+ completed orders.

Founded in 1927 as Bartoloměj Holub Strojírna in Železná Huť, our company has grown over a full century into an ISO 9001 & TÜV certified manufacturer of DIN containers, truck bodies and bespoke steelwork.

Led since 2019 by brothers František and Jan Holub, our workshop combines family accountability with modern CNC equipment — delivering directly to customers in Czechia, Germany, the Netherlands, France and Austria.

4 Historical Epochs

Key archival eras from 1915 through 2027 — click any photograph to inspect full-size.

  1. Epoch 01 · The Founder
    1915–1939

    Bartoloměj Holub founds the workshop & early agricultural machines

    Craftsmanship rooted in formal machine-fitter training in Týn nad Vltavou grew into an independent engineering workshop in Železná Huť building patented machinery and vehicle bodywork.

    • 1915Bartoloměj Holub completes machine-fitter apprenticeship with honours in Týn nad Vltavou.
    • 1917Passes the Prague qualification exam for independent steam boiler operation.
    • 1927Founds Bartoloměj Holub Strojírna in Železná Huť near Nepomuk (agricultural machines, threshers, engines, bicycles).
    • 1930–1939Patented clover huller with fan and sieves; expansion into bodywork, suspension and oxy-acetylene welding.
  2. Epoch 02 · Post-War Years
    1947–1998

    Keeping craft alive under František Holub Sr.

    Post-war hall expansion and fifty years of unbroken family leadership even through state collectivization as associated workshops in Železná Huť.

    • 1947Brick workshop hall built with timber roof truss for heavier machinery work.
    • 1950–1960In-house manufacturing of hullers, blowers, industrial conveyors and workshop machines.
    • 1948–1998Founder’s son František Holub keeps the workshop continuously operating (JZD Kozlovice & ZOD Maňovice).
  3. Epoch 03 · Containers from 1998
    1998–2018

    Private ownership & specialization in DIN containers

    František Holub Jr. reacquired the family workshop in 1998 and shifted production full-scale to Abroll containers, truck bodies and steel structures in Záhoří.

    • 1998František Holub Jr. buys back the company into private family ownership.
    • 2003KOVO-HOLUB Nepomuk s.r.o. incorporated; launch of DIN 30722 Abroll container lines & ISO 9001 quality system.
  4. Epoch 04 · Today & Third Generation
    2019–2027

    CNC modernization, European export & 100 years

    Brothers František and Jan Holub lead 20 specialists producing custom DIN containers, aluminium/steel truck bodies and precision assemblies for five European markets.

    • 2019Third family generation (František & Jan Holub) assumes leadership and grows exports to DE, NL, FR and AT.
    • 2025Major investment in Bystronic Hämmerle 150 3P CNC press brake (160 t) and Pegas 300×320 band saw.
    • 2027100th anniversary of unbroken engineering production founded in 1927.
